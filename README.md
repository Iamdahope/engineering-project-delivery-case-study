# Engineering Project Delivery Case Study

## Project Overview

This case study demonstrates structured delivery of an engineering project with budget control, risk management, schedule tracking and stakeholder coordination.

Budget Range: ~£200,000  
Delivery Environment: Operational infrastructure setting  

---

## Objective

Deliver project scope within approved budget and schedule while maintaining governance compliance and stakeholder alignment.

---

## Scope Definition

- Engineering works coordination
- Supplier management
- Cost tracking and reporting
- Risk and issue monitoring
- Governance documentation support

---

## Project Controls Applied

- RAID log management
- Budget tracking and variance monitoring
- Milestone tracking against baseline
- Stakeholder update reporting
- Stage gate documentation support

---

## Risk Management Approach

Risks were identified, scored and monitored using structured risk matrices. Escalation procedures were defined for high-impact risks.

---

## Lessons Learned

- Early risk identification reduces delivery disruption.
- Clear stakeholder reporting improves decision-making.
- Structured documentation strengthens governance assurance.
